# Servo cabling

Servo hardware - buy for 12 servos:
 * 2.4m of cable (4 conductors - cable [here](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/str-pvc4x0.34/cabluri-pt-lanturi-energet-neecranate/helukabel/49574/) has all 4 in one)
 * 1 connector ([MicroFit 2x2](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/mx-43025-0408/conectori-semnal-raster-3-00mm/molex/43025-0408/)) 
 * 6 crimp ([MicroFit](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/mx-43030-0007/conectori-semnal-raster-3-00mm/molex/43030-0007/))
 
Hub hardware - constructed in pairs of two servos, 5 copies
 * 0.2m of cable (8 conductors)
 * 2 connector ([MicroFit 2x2](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/mx-43020-0401/conectori-semnal-raster-3-00mm/molex/43020-0401/))
 * 12 crimp ([MicroFit](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/mx-43031-0007/conectori-semnal-raster-3-00mm/molex/43031-0007/))
 * 2 connector for Servo Hub ([Dupont 1x3](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/65039-034elf/conectori-semnal-pas-2-54mm/amphenol-communications-solutions/))
 * 8 dupont crimps ([Link](https://www.tme.eu/ro/details/1-104480-2/conectori-semnal-pas-2-54mm/te-connectivity/))
 * 2 standard short servo extensions
 

Microfit connections should be dipped in epoxy.
