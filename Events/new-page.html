Our main activity FTC

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is an international robotics program where students work together to design, build, program, and compete with robots. Each year, teams are given a new challenge that requires their robot to complete specific tasks during matches against and alongside other teams.

How does a match work

  • Matches are played in alliances 2 vs 2.
  • Timel imit for a match is 2:30 minutes from which the first 30 seconds are autonomous so the robots move without human assistance and after 2 minutes of running by one or two drivers from the drive teams.
  • The driving team is composed of one or two drivers that control the robot, a coach that offers guidance during matches, and a HUMAN PLAYER which is a special type of person that can interact with the field in certain ways.
  • Scoring is calculated at the end of the match and a winner is chosen.

Every rule is explained in that year's GAME MANUAL which MUST be read by every member of the team

----→ https://ftc-resources.firstinspires.org/ftc/game/manual

Tehnical departaments strategy

At the start of any sezon, ussually in September, there is the theme annoucement so for us to do the robot we must follow what's called an engineering process:

  1. Analizing the game’s theme
  2. Brainstorming and planing
  3. CAD
  4. Manufacturing and constructing
  5. Programming and testing
  6. Optimizing

At First we usually settle for a simple idea that it's most probably going to work and we improve it in the future.

Tasks are than given to every member and we start working.


How to start a CAD project

We work in Autodesk Fusion 360.

Autodesk Fusion 360 is a CAD software used to create 3D models and designs of objects before they are built. It allows users to make parts, combine them into assemblies, and test how they fit together.
