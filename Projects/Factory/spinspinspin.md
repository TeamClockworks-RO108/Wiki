3-board assembly, each designed with 4 layers.
 * 12-18v Supply
 * 15A continous, 25A peak
 * CAN communication
 * USB communication
 * Inline current sensing on all 3 phases
 * 16k PPR Encoder with SPI
 * STM32G474CE 
 * SimpleFOC
