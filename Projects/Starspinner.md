# Description
Planetary Starspinner is a project that allows to modify the 84 RPM goBILDA Yellow Jacket Planetary motor to create either 4200 RPM or 8000 RPM.

From our prototyping and testing, these two speeds are ideal for launching elements in Decode season.

The entire conversion happens inside the planetary housing, allowing you to mount the motor as you would a with a standard goBILDA motor.
